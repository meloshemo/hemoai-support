# Refund Policy

You may request a refund within the first 7 days of purchase subject to the rules of the app store (Google Play or Apple App Store). HemoAI itself does not directly process payments; all refund decisions and timing are governed by the store.

Guidelines
- Submit the refund request via the store order history.
- Access removed after successful refund.
- Abuse (excessive repeat refunds) may lead to restriction of promotional features.

Partial Refunds
- Not generally granted. Store handles proration in eligible regions.

Contact
If you encounter issues during the refund request flow, email: support@hemoai.org.
