# FAQ

Q: What is HemoAI?
A: An app for tracking hemogram/blood parameters and receiving guidance.

Q: What does Premium include?
A: Advanced analytics, unlimited family, daily insights, and priority support.

Q: Is my data safe?
A: We use optional AES encryption for select fields, secure local storage, offline-first design, and export controls.

Q: How do I cancel my subscription?
A: Use your store account subscriptions page (Google Play / App Store). A shortcut is available in the app under Support.

Q: Can I get a refund?
A: Within 7 days per store rules. See REFUND_POLICY.md for details.
