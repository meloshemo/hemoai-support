# Support & SLA

This document outlines our support commitments and response time targets.

- Standard support: first response within 24 business hours.
- Premium (priority) support: first response within 8 business hours.
- Critical issues (crash loops, data loss): best-effort immediate triage.

Channels
- Support portal: https://meloshemo.github.io/hemoai-support/
- Email: support@hemoai.org

Notes
- Response times are targets, not guarantees; real times may vary by volume and time zone.
- Security incidents are handled as priority regardless of plan.
