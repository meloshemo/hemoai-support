## 4. How We Use Data
- Provide personalised diet recommendations and reminders.
- Sync data securely across devices (if enabled).
- Send transactional emails (password reset, receipt) via SendGrid.
- Process payments through Stripe.
- Perform analytics and crash diagnostics only after user opt-in.
- Comply with legal obligations and enforce terms of service.

## 5. Sharing & Transfers
- **Vendors:** Google Firebase, Twilio SendGrid, Stripe (data processing agreements + SCCs in place).
- **International transfers:** Safeguarded via SCCs and additional assessments.
- **No sale of data** to third parties. Anonymised statistics may be published for research.

## 6. Retention
- Health data retained while account active + 24 months, unless deletion requested.
- Transaction records retained 10 years (accounting law).
- Analytics data retained 14 months (if opted in).
- Backups purged on rolling 30-day schedule.

## 7. Security
- AES-256 encryption at rest (device + cloud).
- TLS 1.2+ in transit.
- Role-based access controls, secret rotation, incident response runbook.
- Regular penetration tests and vulnerability scans (at least annually).

## 8. Your Rights
Depending on jurisdiction (KVKK, GDPR), you may request:
- Access, correction, deletion of data.
- Restriction or objection to processing.
- Data portability.
- Withdrawal of consent without affecting prior lawful processing.
Submit requests via privacy@hemoai.org. Responses provided within 30 days.

## 9. Children’s Privacy
HemoAI is not intended for individuals under 16 years of age. If we learn that we have collected data from a child without verifiable parental consent, we will delete it promptly.

## 10. Changes to This Policy
We may update this policy to reflect operational or legal changes. We will notify users via in-app alert or email. Continued use after the effective date constitutes acceptance.
