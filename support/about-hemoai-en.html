Our Mission

HemoAI helps individuals understand laboratory results, stay informed before doctor visits, and maintain healthier routines. We aim to empower patients with actionable insights while strengthening physician-patient communication.

Core Capabilities

Hemogram Insights

Interpret your latest test with reference ranges, trend analysis, and risk indicators.

Personalised Coaching

Receive nutrition and supplement suggestions tailored to iron, B12, vitamin D, and more.

Family & Sharing

Add loved ones, share results securely, or export PDF/Excel reports.

Reminders & Habits

Medication, hydration, appointments, and lab reminders keep you on track.

Collaboration with Healthcare Professionals

HemoAI is not a substitute for licensed medical advice. We collaborate with dietitians, biochemistry specialists and hematology advisors to review educational content and ensure responsible messaging.

Data Security & Privacy

  • AES-256 encryption on device and TLS 1.2+ for sync.
  • User-controlled backup password and offline usability.
  • Compliance with KVKK, GDPR, HIPAA principles for data minimisation and retention.

Read more in our Data Protection Notice.

Artificial Intelligence Transparency

  • Recommendations rely on reference ranges, medical guidelines, and anonymised behavioural patterns.
  • Healthcare professionals regularly audit AI outputs; inaccurate suggestions can be reported.
  • Critical thresholds trigger warnings and redirect users to professional care.
