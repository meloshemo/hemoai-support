Scope of HemoAI

HemoAI is a wellness companion that helps you interpret hemogram results and build healthier routines. The app does not replace your physician. AI-generated insights are for educational purposes only and must not be considered clinical diagnosis or treatment.

  • Share your latest laboratory report with your doctor before changing medication, supplements or diet.
  • Recommendations are automatically generated from your self-reported and synchronised data; they may not reflect your full medical history.
  • HemoAI is not a medical device or emergency response system.

Emergency Guidance

Call emergency services immediately if you experience severe symptoms such as:

  • Chest pain, pressure, or shortness of breath
  • Sudden weakness, fainting, confusion, or vision loss
  • Uncontrolled bleeding or intense abdominal pain

HemoAI does not dispatch medical help, provide ambulance routing, or contact hospitals on your behalf.

Data Accuracy and Responsibility

  • Analysis results depend on the accuracy of the data you enter or synchronise. Always double-check values against your official lab report.
  • Incorrect units, decimal separators, or outdated data can produce misleading insights.
  • You are solely responsible for validating that the app reflects your real laboratory values.

Eligibility

HemoAI is intended for users aged 13 or older. For minors, usage must take place under the supervision of a legal guardian and licensed healthcare professional.

Artificial Intelligence Transparency

Analysis modules rely on clinical literature, anonymised patterns and expert-reviewed rules. The system highlights risk thresholds but does not deliver conclusive medical judgement.

  • AI models are continuously refined with healthcare advisors; incorrect suggestions can be reported and will be reviewed.
  • Interpretations emphasise trends and reference ranges but cannot assess underlying causes or comorbidities.
